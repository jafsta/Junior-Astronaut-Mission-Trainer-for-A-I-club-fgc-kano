Space Academy 📚

Learn the science behind the systems that keep a space outpost alive.

🫁 Life Support

For a four-person crew, this learning model estimates roughly 3.4 kg of oxygen use per day. Real systems recycle and manage cabin air and water.

🛡️ Radiation

Space radiation comes from solar events and galactic cosmic rays. Shielding, habitat placement and storm shelters help reduce exposure.

⚡ Power

Solar arrays generate electricity and batteries store it. Dust, darkness and equipment demand can reduce available power.

🥬 Food Production

Plants need light, water, nutrients and controlled conditions. A greenhouse can supplement stored food but does not instantly replace it.
